# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Design leaders, product leaders and hiring managers evaluating an experienced product designer for complex financial, operational or platform work. They need to understand within a minute that Mateo combines product judgment, systems thinking and enough frontend fluency to work close to implementation.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio of Mateo Espinosa, a product designer based in Bogotá with more than six years across digital products and frontend, and more than five years focused on product design. The site demonstrates how he approaches financial products, merchant platforms, design systems, code and AI-enabled workflows.

## Positioning

A code-aware product designer who makes dense financial and operational systems clear. Mateo has worked on products for Rappi, Credicorp Capital, Kapital Bank, MiBanco and Banca Mifel, with experience spanning onboarding, transactions, credit, investments, factoring, merchant operations and design systems.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: “Selected work” → five real case studies.
- Secondary CTA: “About Mateo” → professional background and capabilities.
- The line a visitor remembers: “Interfaces built like instruments.”
- Belief ladder: (1) Mateo has precise product judgment → (2) he understands complex financial workflows → (3) he can connect design to frontend → (4) he uses AI to improve execution without outsourcing judgment.
- Proof on hand: Rappi Merchants, MiBanco, Banca Mifel, Credicorp Capital and Kapital Bank.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exacting and technically fluent. The portfolio should feel like a carefully maintained product workspace: warm enough to be human, structured enough to communicate rigor, and interactive only where the interaction proves a capability.

## Anti-references

- Generic Awwwards portfolios with oversized names, loud color and decorative motion.
- Terminal aesthetics used as costume instead of a real interaction.
- Template grids of identical project cards with invented outcomes.
- Dense technical decoration that competes with the actual work.

## Design Principles

1. **Real work first.** Every claim connects to a concrete product, responsibility or skill.
2. **The site behaves like a product.** Terminal, command palette and globe are functional demonstrations.
3. **Minimal does not mean empty.** Fine lines, measured spacing and restrained motion create hierarchy.
4. **Code supports design judgment.** React, Next.js and AI tools shorten the distance from decision to prototype.
5. **Degrade with grace.** Reduced motion, coarse pointers and small screens receive a complete composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives, keyboard-complete navigation and dialogs, readable body contrast, and content that remains available without animation.
